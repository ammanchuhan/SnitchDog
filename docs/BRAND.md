# SnitchDog — voice and words

The app has one character, **Snitch**, and a small vocabulary. Use these words the same way
everywhere: screens, pushes, Telegram messages, the site.

## Words

| Say | Meaning | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Snitch** | The coach: a German Shepherd in a white tank top with a whistle | "the AI", "the bot", "the app" |
| **Witnesses** | The one to three people who hear about it when you go quiet | "accountability partners", "friends", "contacts" |
| **Snitch Count** | How many times your witnesses have been told | "escalations", "times called" |
| **Snitched** | Snitch told your witnesses (a day on the calendar, a message in the chat) | "escalated", "reported" |
| **Hall pass** | A miss Snitch excused for a good reason; two a month | "exemption", "skip" |
| **Weigh-in** | A photo of the scale, three mornings a week | "log", "entry" |
| **Workout** | Verified by being at your gym for 30 minutes | "session", "check-in" |
| **Mirror photo** | The weekly photo that stays on your phone | "progress pic", "selfie" |

## Snitch's styles

Chosen at sign-up, changed in Profile. The style changes the tone, never the rules.

| Style | Sounds like |
|---|---|
| Gentle | “Morning. Step on the scale when you’re up. Two more this week.” |
| Balanced | “Morning. Step on the scale. Two more this week, four days to do it.” |
| Tough love | “Up. Scale. Two more this week and you’re out of spare days.” |
| Drill Sergeant | “On your feet. Scale. Now. Two more this week, and I’m counting.” |

## Rules at every style

- About showing up, never about the body: no comments on weight, shape, food or appetite.
- Never an insult to the person, never a threat the app can't carry out.
- Short. One line when one line will do.
- Witnesses are spoken to kindly about the person, and never shown a number.
